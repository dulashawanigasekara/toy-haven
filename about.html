About Toy Haven

A cheerful place for finding toys, games and little gifts that bring more fun into everyday family life.

A Happy Place for Play

Toys for Big Imaginations

Toy Haven is an online toy shop created to make finding toys simple, colourful and enjoyable. Whether you are shopping for a birthday, choosing a gift or simply looking for something new for playtime, our store brings different types of toys together in one easy place.

From racing cars and bath-time favourites to dolls, family games, riding toys and cuddly companions, Toy Haven offers something for different interests and ways of playing.

We want browsing for toys to feel just as fun as receiving them.
