Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. About This Privacy Policy

Toy Haven respects the privacy of our customers and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Toy Haven website, why that information is used and how it is handled.

By using this website, you understand that information may be stored when you use features such as the shopping cart, wishlist, checkout, newsletter, feedback and contact forms.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, Toy Haven may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Delivery address and city
  • Products added to your shopping cart
  • Products saved to your wishlist
  • Order information
  • Newsletter subscriptions
  • Feedback submitted through the website
  • Messages submitted through the Contact Us page

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through the Toy Haven website may be used to:

  • Process and display customer orders
  • Arrange delivery information
  • Manage shopping cart and wishlist items
  • Respond to customer questions and feedback
  • Store newsletter subscriptions
  • Improve the website and customer experience

4. Browser Local Storage

Toy Haven uses browser local storage to remember information such as shopping cart items, wishlist items, orders, newsletter subscriptions, feedback and contact messages.

Local storage saves information within the user's browser so that selected website information can remain available after the page is refreshed.

Users can remove locally stored information by clearing their browser's website data.

5. Payment Information

Toy Haven may allow customers to select payment options during checkout. Card payment functionality on this website is provided only as a demonstration and does not process real card payments.

The website does not store real bank card numbers, security codes or other sensitive card information.

6. Sharing of Information

Toy Haven does not sell customer personal information. Information would only be used when necessary to provide services such as processing an order, arranging delivery or responding to customer enquiries.

7. Protecting Your Information

Reasonable care is taken when handling information submitted through the Toy Haven website. Customers should also avoid submitting passwords, bank PIN numbers or other highly sensitive information through general contact or feedback forms.

8. Children's Privacy

Toy Haven sells products intended for children, but purchases and submission of personal information should be completed by a parent, guardian or responsible adult where appropriate.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, services or information-handling practices change. The latest version will be displayed on this page.
